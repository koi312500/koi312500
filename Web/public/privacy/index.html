This website does not load Google Analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from this website rather than requested from a separate font CDN.

Browser preferences

Your language and color theme are stored in localStorage on your device until you clear this website’s data. They are used to preserve your choices and are not sent to an analytics service. You can delete or block site storage through your browser settings.

Email enquiries and rights

If you email the operator, your email address, message, attachments and reply history are used to respond to your request. Enquiry records are deleted after the enquiry and necessary follow-up are complete unless retention is required by law. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction through the contact below. Only the minimum information needed to confirm the request will be used. Do not send passwords, tokens or sensitive personal information.

External links

Following a link to another service does not transfer a KOI account automatically. That service’s terms and privacy policy apply when you visit it. Account linking through KOI ID requires the separate information-sharing process shown by KOI ID.

Deletion and protection

Electronic enquiry records no longer needed are deleted from the operator’s mailbox and working files; legally required records are kept separately for the required purpose and period. Browser preferences can be removed by clearing site data. The operator limits access to enquiry information and protects account credentials. Requests about GitHub’s own records may also be directed to GitHub.

Contact and changes

Operator and privacy contact: Geonwoo Kim / koi312500@gmail.com. Changes to this policy are published with their effective date. Additional notice or consent is obtained where required.
